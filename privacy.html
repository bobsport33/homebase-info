Privacy policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Homebase is a personal, self-hosted household application. The household administrator controls its server, access, and backups.

Data and use

With your authorization, Homebase reads Google calendar names, IDs, time zones, and selected events, including titles, dates, times, locations, descriptions, and recurrence details. It caches calendar data and stores encrypted authorization tokens on the household server to display and refresh the requested calendar views. It never asks for your Google password or changes Google events.

Selected calendars are visible to authorized household members. Wall-display access requires separate administrator approval; people near the display may see those events. Google data is not sold or used for advertising, marketing, AI training, or transfers to another calendar provider.

Removal and contact

An administrator can use Account → Calendar connections → Disconnect to remove the local Google connection, tokens, and cached calendar records. Homebase also attempts to revoke Google access; you can revoke it in your Google Account connections. Older backups may retain copies until the administrator removes or expires them.

Contact the household administrator who provided your access for questions or data-removal requests.

This public site

This information site contains no household data or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process visitor IP addresses and request logs under GitHub’s privacy statement.
