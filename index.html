Homebase

A personal project for a private household dashboard.

Homebase displays selected Google calendars alongside groceries, meals, notes, and lists. Its Google Calendar connection is read-only: it cannot create, change, or delete Google events.

The household administrator hosts the application and controls access. These public pages provide information about the project; household data stays on the household installation.
